Our Business Units

Canada

Alimentation Couche-Tard is the leader in the convenience store industry and road transportation fuel retail in Canada, with a network of close to 2,100 stores from the Maritimes to Western Canada. This market is divided into three geographic business units. In its home province of Québec, the company operates under its flagship Couche-Tard brand, while the global Circle K brand is present in the other provinces.

United States

Alimentation Couche-Tard has more than 7,300 stores in the United States which are located in 48 states. The U.S. market is divided into 15 geographic business units. Most of the stores are operated under the global Circle K brand, although a number of stores still operate under the Holiday banner in the Northern Tier business unit. Circle K is the largest independent convenience store operator in the U.S. in terms of the number of company-operated stores.

Europe and other regions

In Europe and other regions, Alimentation Couche-Tard operates a network of more than 5,200 full-service (convenience and fuel) and automated (fuel only) stations. The full-service stations are operated under the Circle K banner in Scandinavian countries (Norway, Sweden and Denmark), in the Baltic countries (Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania), as well as in Ireland, and have an important presence in Poland. The automated sites operate under the INGO banner in Sweden and Denmark. Alimentation Couche-Tard is also a wholesaler of transportation fuel and runs a large commercial fuels operation, including terminals and depots. In 2020, the company proudly expanded into the growing Asian market with the purchase of Circle K Hong Kong and its network of close to 400 convenience stores. In 2024, the company also expanded its operations to Belgium, Germany, Luxembourg and the Netherlands with a network close to 2,200 sites.

International footprint

In addition to North America and Europe, Alimentation Couche-Tard has an international footprint of close to 2,700 Circle K stores in 12 other countries and territories (Egypt, Guam, Guatemala, Honduras, Indonesia, Macau, Mexico, Morocco, New Zealand, Saudi Arabia, UAE and Vietnam). Most of these stores are operated under licensing agreements.

Store count in each business unit (as of July 19th, 2026) includes corporate stores, CODO and DODO and affiliated stores.

CODO:
Sites for which the real estate is controlled by Couche-Tard (through ownership or lease agreements) and for which the stores (and/or the gaz stations) are operated by an independent operator in exchange for rent and to which Couche-Tard supplies road transportation fuel though supply contracts. Some of these sites are subject to a franchise agreement, licensing or other similar agreement under one of our main or secondary banners.

DODO:
Sites controlled and operated by independent operators to which Couche-Tard supplies road transportation fuel through supply contracts. Some of these sites are subject to a franchise agreement, licensing or other similar agreement under one of our main or secondary banners.
